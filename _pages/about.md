<p>Hello there! I am Jairo, a PhD candidate in Physics at the <a href="https://illinois.edu/" style="font-weight:bold;text-decoration:none;color:inherit">University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign</a>, working with <a href="https://mechse.illinois.edu/people/profile/sascha" style="font-weight:bold;text-decoration:none;color:inherit">Sascha Hilgenfeldt</a>.

I completed my undergraduate studies at the <a href="https://www.pucp.edu.pe/" style="font-weight:bold;text-decoration:none;color:inherit">Pontificia Universidad Católica del Perú</a> (Lima, Peru).</p>

<p>Feel free to explore my research, browse my publications, or reach out — I am always happy to discuss physics!</p>
