Welcome! I am Jairo, a PhD candidate in Physics at the **University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign** (Urbana-Champaign, USA), working with **Sascha Hilgenfeldt**.
I study how forces and fluctuations shape living systems — from the mechanics of individual cells to collective behavior and population dynamics.

Before Illinois, I earned my master's degrees in physics: at the **Instituto de Física Teórica – UNESP** (São Paulo, Brazil) and at the **Perimeter Institute** (Waterloo, Canada) through the Perimeter Scholars International program, supported by the SAIFR/IFT–Perimeter Fellowship.
My undergraduate degree is from the **Pontificia Universidad Católica del Perú** (Lima, Peru).

Feel free to explore my research, look through my publications, or reach out — I am always happy to talk physics.
