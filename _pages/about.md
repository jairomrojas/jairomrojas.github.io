Welcome! I am Jairo, a PhD candidate in Physics at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (Urbana-Champaign, USA), working with [Sascha Hilgenfeldt](https://mechse.illinois.edu/people/profile/sascha).
I study how forces and fluctuations shape living systems — from the mechanics of individual cells to collective behavior and population dynamics.

Before Illinois, I earned my master's degrees in physics: at the [Instituto de Física Teórica – UNESP](https://www.ift.unesp.br/) (São Paulo, Brazil) and at the [Perimeter Institute](https://perimeterinstitute.ca/) (Waterloo, Canada) through the [Perimeter Scholars International](https://perimeterinstitute.ca/training/masters-program) program, supported by the [SAIFR/IFT–Perimeter Fellowship](https://journeys.ictp-saifr.org/saifr-perimeter-fellowships/).
My undergraduate degree is from the [Pontificia Universidad Católica del Perú](https://www.pucp.edu.pe/) (Lima, Peru).

Feel free to explore my research, look through my publications, or reach out — I am always happy to talk physics.
