Preprint, January 2025.
Flat vertex models cannot encode actin bundles that anchor into the substrate and pull on the basal face.
The shell model on this paper does, and the outlines it predicts are compared with MDCK II measurements.
How that result sits next to the later disk–ring calculation is explained on [How an epithelial cell stores its shape](/projects/epithelial-shape/).
