Published in Nuclear Instruments and Methods A in 2018.
This was my undergraduate research with José Bazo at PUCP.
Samples of silicon and germanium were irradiated at the RP-10 reactor, their gamma spectra were measured, and those spectra were compared with FLUKA.
For photon peaks above one percent intensity, the relative amplitudes agreed with an average uncertainty of about thirteen percent.
That number is the paper's, and it is what the comparison was for: a systematic error bar on activation calculations for these sensor materials.
